Sprout

Free Amazon keyword research for sellers, KDP authors, and Merch designers. One seed becomes the autocomplete list shoppers see.

Add to Chrome View the source

Not affiliated with Amazon. No account. No tracking. The store button uses the listing address in src/project.ts until the published id replaces pfldfgaedkbpgjmfblncbjdcmjcphnpk.

What you can do

  • Expand a seed across letters and digits, with a space or without one, on the Amazon stores in the picker.
  • Limit suggestions to a department such as Books or Kindle.
  • See which words and pairs show up in the list. Those counts are frequency, not search volume.
  • Fill seven KDP boxes of 50 characters, skipping words already in the title or subtitle.
  • Ask for an Amazon result count on selected rows. The label says result count, not search volume.
  • Read related searches and brand names from the Amazon page you already have open.

What it does not do

  • It does not estimate search volume, cost per click, trends, or sales.
  • It does not sign in to Amazon, Seller Central, or a Sprout account.
  • It does not send your keywords to a Sprout server. Autocomplete requests go from your browser to Amazon.
  • It does not hide a competitor until you click exclude.
  • It is not an Amazon product and it is not a reverse lookup of a product code.
